Break the question into parts before you answer. In sub_questions, write 1 to 3 smaller questions that together answer the child's question (one is enough for a simple question). For each, write in finding what the numbered sentences say about it, or "not in the passages". The child never sees sub_questions. Then fill evidence with the ids behind your findings and write answer by putting the findings together. If no part is answered by the passages, evidence is empty.
